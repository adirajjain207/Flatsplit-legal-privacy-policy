FlatSplit - Privacy & Data Deletion Policy Last Updated: October 21, 2026 1. Introduction This privacy policy applies to the FlatSplit mobile application (the "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Aditya Raj Jain, Sagar, Madhya Pradesh, India. FlatSplit helps flatmates track and split shared expenses. This service is provided free of charge and is intended for use as is. 2. Information Collection and Use To let flatmates share expenses and keep data synchronized across devices, the Application collects: Personal Data: Email address, Display Name and profile photo when logging in via Google Sign-In or Email. Optionally, your UPI ID so flatmates can pay you. Flat & Expense Data: Flat name and address, flatmates, expenses (description, amount, date, category, who paid and how it was split), receipt photos you upload, settlements, comments, shopping list items, chores, notices and meter readings. Information you add to a flat is visible to the other members of that flat. We do not collect or store your bank account details, card numbers or UPI PIN. UPI payments happen entirely inside your own UPI app. We do not sell your personal data. 3. Third-Party Services We use secure third-party services that may collect information used to identify you: Google Play Services Firebase Authentication, Firestore Database & Storage (for secure sign-in and sync) Firebase Analytics and Cloud Messaging (for app improvement and notifications) Google AdMob (for showing ads, which may use your device's advertising ID) 4. Account and Data Deletion Request To comply with Google Play Developer Policies regarding user control over their personal data, we provide straightforward options to delete your account and associated records: In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and associated data by navigating to the "Profile" tab inside the Application and selecting "Delete Account". This permanently removes your profile, login credentials, profile photo, UPI ID and preferences. Web/Remote Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the Application or are unable to access the in-app option, you can submit a deletion request via email to: adirajjain207@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FlatSplit account", sent from the email linked to your account. Upon receiving your request via email, we will verify your identity and permanently delete your authentication credentials, profile and personal data from our cloud databases within 7 business days. Shared expense records that other flatmates depend on are kept for their balances to stay correct, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user". 5. Children's Privacy The Application is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. 6.
